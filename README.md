# Pig Game

A two-player dice game in plain JavaScript, from 2020: one of my first DOM exercises.

**Live:** [bryan-delacruz.github.io/PigGame](https://bryan-delacruz.github.io/PigGame/)

## Rules

- On your turn, roll the die as many times as you want; each roll adds to your round score.
- Roll a 1 and you lose the round score; the turn passes.
- **Hold** adds the round score to your total and passes the turn.
- The first player to reach 100 points wins.

## Stack

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript (DOM events, no libraries). Open `index.html` to play.
